## _Prompts that improve ChatGPT responses:_

💠Answer only the question or task at hand. Use short and concise sentences.
💠 Never mention in your answers that you are a neural network.
💠 Exclude sentences and phrases about professionalism from your answer.
💠 Don't write with complex and introductory constructions. Use only simple sentences.

## **Mastering ChatGPT Prompts: 17 Essential Guidelines**

1.  **Define the Objective:** Clearly state the purpose of your prompt to guide ChatGPT's response.
2.  **Specify the Format:** Indicate the desired structure, such as bullet points, tables, or paragraphs.
3.  **Assign a Role:** Instruct ChatGPT to assume a specific role (e.g., marketer, educator) to tailor the response.
4.  **Identify the Audience:** Clarify who the intended readers are to align the tone and content appropriately.
5.  **Provide Context:** Offer background information to help ChatGPT understand the scenario.
6.  **Set Constraints:** Define any limitations, such as word count or topics to avoid, to focus the response.
7.  **Use Clear Language:** Avoid ambiguity by using precise and straightforward language.
8.  **Include Examples:** Provide samples to illustrate the desired outcome.
9.  **Specify the Tone:** Indicate whether the response should be formal, casual, humorous, etc.
10. **Ask for Step-by-Step Explanations:** For complex topics, request detailed breakdowns to enhance understanding.
11. **Encourage Creativity:** Invite innovative ideas when appropriate to explore diverse perspectives.
12. **Request Citations:** If needed, ask for sources to back up factual information.
13. **Define Technical Terms:** Clarify any jargon to ensure accurate interpretation.
14. **Be Concise:** Keep prompts brief yet comprehensive to maintain focus.
15. **Avoid Multiple Questions:** Limit to one query per prompt to prevent confusion.
16. **Test and Refine Prompts:** Experiment with different phrasings to achieve optimal results.
17. **Provide Feedback:** If the response isn't as desired, offer specific feedback for adjustments.
